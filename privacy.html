Yard Sale Flipper: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Yard Sale Flipper ("the game") is made by Rinshad Nadukkandi. This policy explains what the game does with your information.

What stays on your device

Your progress (money, items, upgrades, museum, Daily Sale results) and your settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us.

Purchases

The game offers a one-time "Full Game" in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

To check whether you own the Full Game and to restore it on a new device, the game uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). RevenueCat receives your purchase history for this app and an anonymous identifier it creates. The identifier is not your name, email or Apple ID. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Photos

If you choose to share a before-and-after card and pick "Save Image", the game asks for permission to add that picture to your photo library. The game never reads your photos.

Sharing

When you share a card or a Daily Sale score, the text and image go to the app you pick in the iOS share sheet, under that app's own terms.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • We don't sell or share personal data

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue at https://github.com/devrinshadnk/yardsaleflipper/issues
